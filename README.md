AI-Based Video & Data Optimization
This repository documents workflows and methodologies for AI-driven video processing, focusing on high-efficiency data encoding and frame rate enhancement.

Key Features & Skills Demonstrated:

AI Upscaling & Interpolation: Utilizing Topaz Video AI (Proteus model) for 2x resolution upscaling and 60 FPS conversion to enhance visual data clarity.

High-Efficiency Data Encoding: Implementing HandBrake workflows for H.265 (HEVC) compression to optimize storage and transmission without quality loss.

Advanced Post-Production: Integrating visual enhancements and motion blur rendering using Adobe After Effects 2023 with industry-standard plugins (Magic Bullet Looks, RSMB).
